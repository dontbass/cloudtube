CloudTube is an Invidious client for Android and Android TV with the familiar mobile YouTube layout. A fork of Clipious.

<ul>
<li>Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, You — bottom navigation like mobile YouTube</li>
<li>Tabs: Music, Live, News, Sports, Gaming, Podcasts, Movies</li>
<li>Vertical swipeable Shorts with preloading</li>
<li>Threaded comments in a bottom sheet</li>
<li>Invidious account: subscriptions, history, playlists</li>
<li>SponsorBlock, video proxying, downloads, background audio, picture-in-picture</li>
<li>In-app updates</li>
</ul>

Works with any Invidious server; optional CloudTube server add-ons provide a personal feed, curated Shorts and yt-dlp comments.
